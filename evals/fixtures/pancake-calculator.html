Method

  1. Whisk the dry ingredients (amounts above) in a large bowl.
  2. Whisk the dairy blend, eggs, and melted butter in another.
  3. Fold together until just combined — your fluff setting assumes visible streaks of flour remain.
  4. Rest the batter for the time shown above, unless you told us you're in a hurry.
  5. Cook on a medium griddle until bubbles set; flip once.

Why does tang change the soda? See the methodology page for the acid budget — the short version is that yogurt and lemon both spend soda, and the calculator balances the books so your pancakes rise instead of tasting soapy.
